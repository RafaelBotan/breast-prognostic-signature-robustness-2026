# Ethics and data protection

This methodological study used existing public, deidentified breast-cancer cohorts. The authors did not recruit participants, contact patients, or redistribute individual-level observations. Institutional ethics review was therefore not required for the present secondary analysis.

This repository contains only analysis code, aggregate or derived result tables, and figures. It contains no names, record numbers, dates of birth, clinical report text, credentials, or patient-level expression or survival rows.

Users who obtain source cohort data must comply with the access and reuse terms of GEO, GDC, cBioPortal, EGA, and the original studies.
